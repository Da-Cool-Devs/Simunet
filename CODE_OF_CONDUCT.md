# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want the Simunet community to be open, respectful, and useful to everyone. Contributors, users, and maintainers agree to take part in an environment free from harassment and discrimination.

## Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Giving useful feedback
- Accepting mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what helps the project and community
- Respecting different viewpoints and experience levels

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, inappropriate sexual attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and activity that violate this code of conduct. They may also restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the community.

## Reporting

To report a concern, open a private security advisory or contact the repository maintainers through GitHub. Repository maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly.
