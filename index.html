WHAT YOU WILL PRACTICE

The real jobs behind every website

BUILD

Design a data center

Choose rack frames and install web, database, storage, backup, and sign-in servers, plus switches, routers, and load balancers.

OPERATE

Keep the lights on

Balance a real budget: hardware costs money, customers pay, and power and protection have running costs. Scale up before your servers are overwhelmed.

DEFEND

Survive incidents

Handle power outages, traffic floods, password attacks, disk failures, and viral traffic spikes, then learn what would have prevented them.

Your data stays with you. Everything in SimuNet runs in your browser. The camera lab is a simulation: photos stay on your device and are never uploaded.
